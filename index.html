About me

I am an applied mathematician working where mathematics meets biology. Most of my work models infectious diseases, along with predator–prey and eco-epidemic interactions, using ordinary, fractional-order, delay and stochastic differential equations. My current focus is on spatio-temporal models: how diffusion, spatial heterogeneity and incubation delays create patterns and change the course of an outbreak.

Before joining IIT Kanpur, I was an Assistant Professor at SR University, Warangal, and a Postdoctoral Fellow at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Bengaluru. I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from IIEST Shibpur in 2025.
